# Product

## Audience

People considering Cem for internships, collaborations, or technical conversation. They should understand who he is, what he has shipped, and where his current technical foundations stand without operating an interface first.

## Purpose

A Turkish-first, person-led portfolio built around real products and inspectable experiments. The homepage introduces Cem plainly and presents GündemAI, the Bike Demand temporal benchmark, the Fashion-MNIST NumPy experiment, and WC2026 with evidence, direct links, limitations, and deeper case pages. SleepInfo remains in the repository as an unfeatured prior case page; its published model metrics are explicitly marked as not independently validated.

## Positioning

Cem is an Eskişehir Osmangazi University Mathematics and Computer Sciences student strengthening statistics and classical machine-learning foundations and studying deep-learning fundamentals through experiments. He uses AI extensively for research, orchestration, production, and critique. The site does not imply handwritten framework mastery or expert status.

## Content hierarchy

1. Cem's photograph, name, literal positioning, and direct CV/contact paths.
2. GündemAI as the primary Android product published on Google Play, with a real store screenshot and a direct listing link visible in the opening viewport.
3. Bike Demand as a temporal machine-learning benchmark, with its held-out MAE comparison and limitations visible.
4. Fashion-MNIST as a NumPy experiment comparing linear softmax and a 128–64 ReLU MLP, with official test accuracy, one-seed/split scope, synthetic-noise limits, and AI assistance visible.
5. WC2026 as a tertiary educational statistical simulation, with its output chart and repository link. It uses feature weights, Poisson, and Monte Carlo; it is not trained machine learning.
6. Selected technical writing, education, contact links, and a minor link to AI İnceleme Masası. SleepInfo stays as an archived case route outside the homepage selection.

## Preserved product surface

The `/work` routes, blog routes, sitemap, CV, profile image, GitHub, LinkedIn, email, GündemAI Play listing, Bike Demand repository/report, Fashion-MNIST repository/frozen report, SleepInfo live product/repository, WC2026 repository, `/ai-inceleme-masasi`, and `POST /api/review/runs` remain available.

## Constraints

No X-Ray mode, hidden inspection UI, evidence desk, ledger, engineering harness, simulator, agent characters, fake telemetry, terminal treatment, decorative metrics, or stale test counts on the homepage. No unverified claims. No WC2026 live-demo link while it redirects to authentication.

## Quality bar

Semantic landmarks and headings, visible keyboard focus, 44 × 44 pixel interactive targets, reduced-motion support, meaningful product evidence in the initial mobile viewport, no document-level horizontal overflow at 320px/390px/desktop/200% zoom, loaded images, and zero Axe violations.
